Support

Something not working, or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub with your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened.

Steps aren't showing

  • Open iOS Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Meowtrek and turn on Steps, Walking + Running Distance, Flights Climbed and Active Energy.
  • In Meowtrek, go to Settings › Connected devices › Apple Health › Refresh.
  • Using another tracker? Turn on Apple Health sync in that tracker's own app, or link Fitbit or Garmin directly.

My route wasn't recorded

Allow Location for Meowtrek in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services. Very short treks (under about 30 metres and a minute) aren't saved.

Restore my Plus subscription

Open the Plus screen and tap Restore. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID.

The cat coach says AI isn't available

On-device AI needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on. Otherwise Meowtrek uses the cloud coach, which needs an internet connection. You can switch between them in Settings › AI coach.

Add a friend

Ask your friend to tap Share card in the Social tab and send you the link. Opening the link on your iPhone adds them; share yours again whenever you want them to see your latest week.
